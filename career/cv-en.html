Profile

Clear requirements for developers and simpler workflows for customers: that is my contribution as a Product Owner. Nine years at iPaper, from support to product development, give me an understanding of user needs and the technology behind them. Working closely with developers and UX, I helped improve automation and interfaces and explained releases through videos, demos and release notes. My practical AI experience includes websites, an app and reusable workflows built with Claude, Codex and AI loops.

Selected achievements at iPaper

01

More customer needs covered by one shared solution

More data sources and customer needs could be supported by one shared solution for shoppable PDF catalogues. Expanded Enrichment Automation in close collaboration with a developer over around two years.

02

A simpler way to change product images

A slow product-image workflow became simpler with a new interface and asynchronous processing. Helped define and refine the solution; after two iterations, users were satisfied.

03

From releases to practical guidance

Made new features understandable for customers and colleagues through internal product updates, release notes, customer how-to videos and weekly demos.

04

Price formatting customers asked for

Customers gained control over currency placement and trailing zeros. Specified both options, which shipped and received positive feedback through Customer Care.
