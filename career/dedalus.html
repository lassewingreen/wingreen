Dear Dedalus team,

I like understanding what people need from software and working out how to make it useful to them. That interest has taken me from customer support through implementation to product ownership during close to nine years at iPaper. I'm applying for the Product Owner role at Dedalus because I would like to bring that experience to software that healthcare professionals depend on in their daily work.

At iPaper, I specified the full project to improve the e-commerce checkout experience and product view. I worked through the requirements with developers and stayed involved in the discussions throughout. My earlier implementation work helped me understand how individual product decisions fit into a customer's wider setup. I enjoy getting into those details and making them clear enough for a team to work with.

Accessibility has also been an important part of my product work. I worked on a full accessibility suite for Flipbook and specified an accessibility suite for Horizon, a new addition to iPaper's products. For Horizon, I also specified an energy-rating system and numerous other product issues. These are different kinds of work, but they share something I care about: making complicated functionality understandable and usable.

I have worked closely with developers and taken part in regular Scrum ceremonies. Your emphasis on clear user stories, refinement and ongoing clarification appeals to me. At Dedalus, I would bring a practical approach to those conversations: understand the need, make open questions visible and work through the scope with the team. I would use feedback from users and colleagues to help keep the backlog focused on what matters most.

Years in support taught me to listen before jumping to a solution, and to explain technical things in language people can use. Implementation added experience with automation, data feeds, Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager. I would bring that combination of customer understanding and technical curiosity to the collaboration between your users, stakeholders and development team.

What attracts me to Dedalus is the opportunity to apply that approach where dependable, usable software has a direct purpose in people's working lives. I would welcome a conversation about your product area, the team's challenges and how I could contribute.

Best regards,Lasse Wingreen
